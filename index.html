      How a scoop earns its rank

      Three tests. No hype.

      1. Is the source named? A name, a document or a recording beats "people say".
      2. Is it on the record? Someone with authority said it, in public.
      3. Did it hold up? We come back and check. Rumors never get a fact label until they earn it.

      A rumor can rank high for being first and interesting. It keeps its rumor label until it is confirmed.
